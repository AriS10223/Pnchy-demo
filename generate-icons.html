Pnchy Icon Generator

Generates full-bleed icons with no card border — fixing the white border bug on iOS and Android home screens.

After downloading:
1. Copy the 3 files into pnchy-demo/, replacing the old ones.
2. In sw.js, change pnchy-demo-v2 → pnchy-demo-v3 to force cache refresh.
3. In your browser: DevTools → Application → Service Workers → Unregister, then hard-reload.
4. Add to home screen again to see the fixed icon.
